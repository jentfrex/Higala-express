About Us

Learn more about Higala Express — your trusted superapp for delivery and e-commerce in Cagayan de Oro.

Our Story

Higala Express was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to connect people, merchants, and riders through seamless technology. Born in Cagayan de Oro, we understand the unique needs of our community and strive to provide fast, reliable, and affordable services that make everyday life easier.

As a local superapp, we empower small businesses, create livelihood opportunities for riders, and bring convenience to every household. Our platform is built by CDOans, for CDOans — with a deep commitment to serving our community with integrity and excellence.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become the leading and most trusted superapp platform providing fast, reliable delivery and e-commerce services across the region.

Our Mission

Empowering local merchants, riders, and customers through seamless technology, transparent pricing, and efficient logistics solutions.
